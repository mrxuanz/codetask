# Support

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/mrxuanz/codetask/issues) for reproducible bugs, feature
requests, and setup questions. Search existing issues first and include your operating system,
Node version, codetask commit or release, selected provider, and relevant redacted logs.

Provider account, billing, or upstream CLI incidents should be reported to that provider. Please
do not post credentials, access tokens, private source code, or other secrets in an issue.

For suspected vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and avoid public disclosure until
a fix or mitigation is available.
